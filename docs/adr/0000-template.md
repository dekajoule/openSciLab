# NNNN. The decision, said as a rule

* Status: proposed
* Date: YYYY-MM-DD
* Supersedes: – (or the ADR this one replaces)

## Context

What situation forces a decision: the problem, the constraints (hardware, Python, Qt, users'
files, contributors), what was tried. Facts, not the solution.

## Decision

What we do, as concrete rules a reviewer can check ("X never imports Y", "every Z goes through W").

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, what it costs; the follow-up work and the exceptions
that are allowed (and how they are marked).

## Alternatives considered

The other options and why they lost, one or two lines each.

## Enforced by

The tests, CI checks or review rules that keep the decision true (`tests/test_...py`). "Review"
alone is allowed, but say what the reviewer looks for.
